About Mir Movil Lab Service

Founded in Miami on three principles — care, accuracy and access — to remove the waiting room from laboratory testing.

Why we started

Too many patients postpone the blood work their doctor ordered, not because they refuse the test, but because getting to a collection centre means a morning off work, a parking garage and an hour in a crowded room. For an elderly or homebound patient it can mean not going at all.

Mir Movil Lab Service was built to close that gap. We travel to the patient, collect the specimen under the same protocols a hospital laboratory follows, and deliver it to the processing lab the same day. The patient keeps their morning; the physician still gets the result.

Listen to our welcome message

A short spoken overview of what happens during a home visit, for anyone who would rather listen than read.

Read the transcript

Welcome to Mir Movil Lab Service. We bring licensed phlebotomists to your home, office, or care facility anywhere in Miami-Dade and Broward County.

When you book a visit, your phlebotomist confirms your identity and your doctor's order, collects the sample, labels it in front of you, and delivers it to the processing laboratory the same day. A routine visit takes about twenty minutes.

If you are nervous about needles, tell us when we arrive. We will take the time you need.

Our core values

  • Patient comfort

    A calm, unhurried draw in familiar surroundings, with time for anyone who is anxious about needles.

  • Precision

    HIPAA protocols, chain of custody labelling and temperature controlled transport on every single specimen.

  • Access

    We meet you at home, at the office or at a care facility, on a schedule that fits around your day.
